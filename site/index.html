What we do

One firm for the decisions that shape a business

Accountability

Named people, reviewed work

A named owner in every area

Your company is allotted to a named professional in each area that applies to you: GST, TDS, PF and ESI, corporate compliance, legal and assurance. The same person stays with your company until a handover is formally recorded.

Three levels of review

Work is prepared, checked and approved by different people before it reaches you. Nobody approves their own work.

Prepared→Checked by a supervisor→Approved by a manager

Applicability reviewed monthly

Where a compliance does not yet apply to you, such as PF or ESI, we do not simply close it. We reassess it every month and start monitoring as soon as it applies.

Documents come only from your registered contacts

At onboarding we record the email addresses and phone numbers of your promoters, partners or directors and your nominated finance contacts. Any document or instruction from your organisation must come from one of those registered sources. Anything else is not approved or acted on. This protects you against forged or unauthorised instructions.
